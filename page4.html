In the quiet glow of this magical night,

I send a wish wrapped in starlight bright.

For you, my friend, so precious and true,

Here is the warmth I am sending to you.

May you and your family, day after day,

Share meals filled with laughter and chase cares away.

May your tables be blessed, your plates always full,

Eating well, living well, in sweet peaceful lull.

I know that life holds paths we cannot foresee,

And storms may roll in on a turbulent sea.

There will be days when the challenges grow,

And shadows fall long in the valley below.

But stand tall, my friend, when the cold winds start,

Face every trial with a courageous, brave heart.

Walk honestly, purely, with kindness inside,

Let a good, noble spirit forever abide.

For within your own soul lies the strength to endure,

To solve every problem and keep your mind pure.

You will find the solutions, you will find the way,

To turn every night into glorious day.

So live this life fully, stand strong and stand free,

Belated happy birthday, Estelle, the best friend to me.
